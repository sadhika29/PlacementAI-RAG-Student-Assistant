APTITUDE TEST PREPARATION

Common aptitude test areas include:

1. Quantitative aptitude.
2. Logical reasoning.
3. Verbal ability.
4. Data interpretation.
5. Basic problem solving.

Students should practice arithmetic, percentages, ratios, averages, probability, time and work, time and distance, logical puzzles and data interpretation.

Regular timed practice can help students improve speed and accuracy.

Students should analyze incorrect answers and revise weak topics.
