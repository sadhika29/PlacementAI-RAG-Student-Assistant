INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES

Internships provide students with practical exposure to professional environments.

Students should select internships relevant to their career interests where possible.

Before accepting an internship, students should understand:

1. Organization name.
2. Internship duration.
3. Work location.
4. Role and responsibilities.
5. Stipend if applicable.
6. Working hours.
7. Required skills.
8. Reporting structure.

Students should maintain professional conduct during internships and complete assigned responsibilities.
