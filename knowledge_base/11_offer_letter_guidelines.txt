OFFER LETTER GUIDELINES

Students receiving employment offers should carefully review the offer letter.

Important details may include:

1. Job title.
2. Location.
3. Compensation.
4. Joining date.
5. Probation period.
6. Employment conditions.
7. Required documents.
8. Notice period.
9. Company policies.

Students should clarify questions with the recruiting organization before accepting an offer.

Students should maintain copies of important employment documents.
