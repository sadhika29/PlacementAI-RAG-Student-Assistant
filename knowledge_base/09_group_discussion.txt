GROUP DISCUSSION GUIDELINES

Group discussions may be used by recruiters to evaluate communication and teamwork.

Students should:

1. Listen carefully to other participants.
2. Present ideas clearly.
3. Support arguments with logical reasoning.
4. Avoid interrupting unnecessarily.
5. Respect different opinions.
6. Encourage constructive discussion.
7. Maintain professional body language.

The goal is not simply to speak the most. Quality of ideas, communication and teamwork are important.
