TECHNICAL INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

Technical interviews may evaluate fundamental knowledge relevant to the student's degree and the job role.

For computer science students, important areas may include:

1. Programming fundamentals.
2. Data structures and algorithms.
3. Object-oriented programming.
4. Database management systems.
5. Operating systems.
6. Computer networks.
7. Software engineering.
8. Basic machine learning when relevant.

Students should be able to explain the projects listed on their resumes.

Candidates should understand the technologies they claim as skills.

Students should practice solving programming problems and explaining their approach clearly.
