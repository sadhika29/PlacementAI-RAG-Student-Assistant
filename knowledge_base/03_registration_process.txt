PLACEMENT REGISTRATION PROCESS

Students interested in campus placements must complete the registration process announced by the Training and Placement Cell.

The registration process generally includes:

1. Logging into the placement portal.
2. Entering personal information.
3. Entering academic information.
4. Uploading the latest resume.
5. Uploading required academic documents.
6. Providing contact information.
7. Selecting eligible recruitment opportunities.
8. Confirming the registration before the deadline.

Students should verify all information before submitting the registration form.

Late registration may not be accepted after the company-specific deadline.
