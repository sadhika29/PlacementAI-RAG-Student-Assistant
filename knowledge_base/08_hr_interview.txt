HR INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

HR interviews may evaluate communication, attitude, teamwork, adaptability and career motivation.

Common questions include:

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. What are your strengths?
3. What are your weaknesses?
4. Why do you want to join our organization?
5. Why should we hire you?
6. Where do you see yourself in five years?
7. Describe a challenging situation you handled.
8. Are you comfortable working in a team?

Students should answer honestly and professionally.

Candidates should research the organization and understand the job role before the interview.
