PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Students must satisfy the eligibility criteria specified by the recruiting company.

General institute-level eligibility requirements include:

1. Minimum CGPA of 7.0 on a 10-point scale.
2. Students should not have active academic backlogs at the time of registration.
3. Students must satisfy the academic requirements specified by the recruiting organization.
4. Students must maintain the required attendance according to institute regulations.
5. Students must complete placement registration.
6. Students must submit accurate academic records.

Individual companies may specify additional requirements such as branch, CGPA, programming skills, communication skills or graduation year.
