PLACEMENT RULES

Students participating in placement activities must follow professional conduct.

Students must:

1. Attend recruitment activities on time.
2. Carry their college identification card when required.
3. Dress appropriately for interviews.
4. Maintain professional communication.
5. Provide truthful information in resumes and application forms.
6. Follow instructions given by the placement coordinators.
7. Avoid disruptive behavior during recruitment activities.

Students should not submit false academic information or fabricated certificates.

Recruitment participation may be restricted if a student violates placement rules.
