RESUME GUIDELINES

Students should prepare a clear and professional resume.

A resume should generally contain:

1. Full name and contact information.
2. Career objective or professional summary.
3. Educational qualifications.
4. Technical skills.
5. Projects.
6. Internships or practical experience.
7. Certifications.
8. Achievements or relevant extracurricular activities.

Students should avoid spelling and grammatical errors.

The resume should be concise and preferably limited to one or two pages for entry-level applications.

Students should customize the resume according to the job description when appropriate.

Students should not include false information.
