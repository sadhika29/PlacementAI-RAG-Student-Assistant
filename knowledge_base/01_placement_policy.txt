PLACEMENT POLICY

The Training and Placement Cell coordinates campus recruitment activities for eligible students.

Students participating in campus placement must complete the placement registration process before the announced deadline.

Students are expected to maintain professional conduct during recruitment activities.

Students must attend company pre-placement talks, tests, interviews and other recruitment activities when registered for a company.

Students who accept an offer must follow the placement cell's joining and documentation procedures.

Students must provide accurate academic and personal information during registration.

The placement cell may modify recruitment schedules depending on company requirements.
