PLACEMENT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is the general minimum CGPA?
A: The general institute-level minimum CGPA is 7.0, although companies may specify different requirements.

Q: Can students with active backlogs participate?
A: The general placement policy requires students to have no active academic backlogs, although company-specific requirements may differ.

Q: Should I prepare my resume?
A: Yes. Students should maintain a concise and accurate professional resume.

Q: What should I study for technical interviews?
A: Students should revise programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems and other subjects relevant to the job role.

Q: How should I prepare for HR interviews?
A: Practice common HR questions, improve communication skills and research the organization and job role.

Q: What should I do before an interview?
A: Review your resume, research the organization, revise relevant technical concepts and prepare questions for the interviewer.

Q: Can company requirements differ?
A: Yes. Recruiting organizations may define additional eligibility and skill requirements.
